# Day 30 · Rest, reread, plan the next two weeks

Rest is part of the plan: an interview the day after a 12-hour cram goes worse than one after a quiet day.

## Morning (1 h, no more)
- Reread the **must-know answers** in the roadmap (section 9) and the "Say it out loud" questions of days 5, 6, 11, 12, 20 and 22. Say each answer once. Do not study anything new.
- Reread your six stories and your 60-second intro (day 24). Say the intro once.

## Plan the next two weeks (1 h)
Write a one-page table, one row per firm you applied to:

| Firm | Stage | Next step and date | What they test (from your recruiter or public sources) | Prep for it |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Then:
- **Before each interview:** 30 minutes on that firm's style (Jane Street: clean coding and probability; Citadel Securities: low-latency deep dive; Optiver and IMC: mental maths, probability, market-making games), one timed build drill from day 29, your intro once.
- **After each interview, the same day:** write down every question you were asked and how you answered. Anything weak goes on the weak list. Ask for a mock in the thread on the topics that came up.
- **Keep warm daily (45 min):** one build drill or 2 LeetCode problems, 10 probability problems, 15 minutes of Zetamac.

## Afternoon
Off. Sleep well.

**Done when:** the table exists and every row has a next step with a date.
